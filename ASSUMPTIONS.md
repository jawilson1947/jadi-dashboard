## Resolved by discovery (2026-09-17)

**2026-09-18 (J. Wilson) — A-22, `LastCleared` resolves to a semester.** `tblStudent.LastCleared` matches `tblOUSA.JADI_TradName` / `JADI_LeapName` on *any* row, not only `isCurrent` / `wasCurrent`, so where a record matches, that semester's full metadata is derivable (name, academic year, begins/ends, drop date, worksheet folder, nightly census and cleared); `'XX0000'` is the never-cleared sentinel. Decisions: (a) the sprint Compare tab benchmarks against same-season prior semesters' nightly `tblOUSA.FinanciallyCleared` — the official figure under A-2 — not against a `LastCleared` count, which is the rolled-to population (1,153 vs 1,012 for FA2026); (b) the general term-code → semester resolver, and §9.3 receivables by semester which needs it, are **deferred to Phase 4**; term codes outside the current/previous pair render raw until then.

- A-4 view name → `dbo.VIEW_OURM_ACAD`. A-16 term keys → `tblOUSA.JADI_TradName` / `JADI_LeapName`. Q4 worksheet location → `tblOUSA.WorksheetFolder` per semester. Q5 → all `VIEW_OURM_*` views are already scoped to `tblOUSA.isCurrent = 1`; historical queries must go to `tblStudent.LastCleared` / `tblOUSA` instead.
- New operational constraint: `VIEW_OURM_FCA` and `VIEW_OURM_STATS` take 40–120+ s per query on staging; dashboards must read snapshots only (PLAN Phase 2 is a prerequisite for Phase 6, not an optimization).
